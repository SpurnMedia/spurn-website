Accessibility

SPURN is working toward an accessible editorial product that can be navigated and understood across devices and input methods.

Last updated 5 October 2026
TrustCorrectionsAI & Editorial UsePrivacyTerms

Our target

SPURN uses WCAG 2.2 Level AA as its accessibility target. We do not yet make a formal claim that every page fully conforms to WCAG 2.2 AA; a final full-site accessibility acceptance pass remains part of the build process.

Current measures

The current site includes semantic headings and landmarks, a skip link, visible keyboard focus, keyboard-operable mobile navigation, responsive layouts and reduced-motion handling. We aim to provide meaningful alternative text where images carry editorial meaning.

Known status

The site is still being refined. Accessibility issues can exist even when automated or checklist-based tests pass, particularly across assistive technologies, browsers and real-world reading conditions.

Report a barrier

If something on SPURN is difficult to access, email hello@spurnmedia.com. If possible, include the page URL, what you were trying to do, the barrier you encountered, and the browser, device or assistive technology you were using.

Alternative access

If a published item is inaccessible to you in its current form, contact us and we will make a reasonable effort to provide the information in another usable form.
